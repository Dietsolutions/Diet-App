Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Diet Plan & Tracker ("we", "our", "app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account data: username/email, password (hashed), OAuth profile info (name, avatar) if you sign in with Google or Apple.
  • Health data: weight, height, age, gender, dietary preferences, activity level, meal logs, calorie/macro targets, water intake, and other nutrition/wellness information you enter.
  • Usage data: meal plan generations, feature interactions, session length (anonymized via PostHog if you opt in).
  • Error data: crash reports and stack traces (Sentry) — no personal identifiers attached.

2. How We Use Data

  • Generate personalized meal plans using AI (Claude via OpenRouter). Meal plan prompts include your dietary preferences and goals — no raw personal identifiers are sent.
  • Authenticate you and maintain your session.
  • Improve app stability (Sentry crash reporting).
  • Understand feature usage (PostHog — opt-in only).

3. Data Storage

  • Your data is stored on Neon (PostgreSQL), hosted in Singapore (ap-southeast-1).
  • Passwords are bcrypt-hashed. Refresh tokens are stored as SHA-256 hashes.
  • We retain your data until you delete your account, which permanently removes all associated records.

4. Third-Party Services

  • OpenRouter/Anthropic — AI meal plan generation. Prompts include dietary preferences but no names or email addresses.
  • CalorieNinjas — nutrition database lookups for macro validation (ingredient names only).
  • Sentry — error and crash reporting.
  • PostHog — product analytics (opt-in; never captures health data).
  • Vercel — hosting and serverless function execution.
  • Neon — PostgreSQL database hosting.

5. Your Rights

  • Delete account: available in-app. All your data is permanently removed.
  • Export data: contact us and we'll provide a JSON export of your data.
  • Opt out of analytics: disable "Analytics" in app settings at any time.

6. Children

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we'll delete it.
