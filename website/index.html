The honest part

How a meal earns its place on your plan

AI is great at ideas and bad at arithmetic. So we don't trust it with the numbers.

AI drafts the meal

A meal is generated to fit your slot's calorie and protein budget, with every ingredient in gram quantities.

The database checks it

Each ingredient is looked up in a real nutrition database, and the meal's true macros are computed independently.

Off-target? It's fixed

Small drift gets portions rescaled proportionally. Big drift sends the meal back to the AI to be replaced entirely.

The day has to add up

The finished day is checked against your daily budget too — within ±15%, or the largest meal gets swapped.

Most meal apps stop at step 1. This pipeline is the whole reason the app exists.
